Imperial units fixed.

Switching between Meters and Feet now converts every value you have already
entered — stop distance, last stop, END, altitude, the ascent and descent
rate tables, your RMVs and your dive levels. Previously it converted none of
them, so selecting Feet reinterpreted the metric defaults as feet: a 3 m last
stop silently became 3 ft, and the ascent rate bands left a deep dive with no
defined rate over its deepest section.

Gas consumption now follows the depth units. Choosing Feet gives you cubic
feet without having to find a second control, and the RMV units stay where you
put them if you set them yourself.

Every Config field now shows the unit it is in.

The experimental extra-slow ascent rule has been removed. Measured
against every realistic gradient-factor setting it never engaged once, so
the checkbox did nothing; no schedule changes as a result.

Engine 1.23.0. Cross-checked against MultiDeco on a 230 ft trimix dive: same
first stop, same gas switches, stop times within one minute and always on the
conservative side.
